Next steps

1. Download the codes file and commit it to your private data repo as data/codes.json — before printing. If you print codes the app has never heard of, a scan of an unassigned label looks identical to a typo.

2. Print one test sheet on plain paper and hold it over a blank label sheet against a window. If it is off, adjust the margins in "Sheet geometry" — never fix alignment by rescaling at print time.

Print at Actual size / 100% — never "Fit to page", which is the most common cause of labels drifting off their backing. Use pure black, no toner-save and no draft mode. Margins are built into the sheet, so set the printer's own margin to None if it offers the choice. On a colour laser, also set the driver to Black & White / grayscale: composite black mixed from cyan, magenta and yellow can land a hair out of registration, and the module edges are exactly where a scanner is least forgiving.

Buy by specification, not by part number — vendors reuse numbers across completely different products. Match the size and count above, and for the fridge also insist on weatherproof film / polyester rated for laser. Condensation when the door opens in a warm room is what destroys labels; plain paper wicks and smears, and laser toner fuses to film in a way inkjet ink does not.

Put the label on the shoulder or a blank part of the back label — not the foil capsule, which peels away with the foil, and not the very bottom, which sits in whatever pools on the shelf. On a sharply curved shoulder a round label lies flatter than a wide rectangle, which tends to lift at its corners.
