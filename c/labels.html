Next steps

1. Download the codes file and commit it to your private data repo as data/codes.json — before printing. If you print codes the app has never heard of, a scan of an unassigned label looks identical to a typo.

2. Print one test sheet on plain paper. Hold it over a blank label sheet against a window to check alignment before committing real stock.

Print at Actual size / 100% — never "Fit to page", which is the most common cause of labels drifting off their backing. Use pure black, no toner-save and no draft mode.

In a wine fridge the real enemy is condensation when the door opens in a warm room. Use laser-printed polyester (Avery 94503/5520) — an inkjet will not fuse to the film, and plain paper wicks and smears. Put the label on the shoulder or a blank part of the back label: not the foil capsule, which peels, and not the very bottom, which sits in whatever pools on the shelf.
