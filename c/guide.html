Cellar guide

Wine Cellar — how it works

skymath.org/c
Every bottle wears a QR sticker. Scan it with the phone’s normal camera — there is nothing to install. Bookmark the address above, or add it to your Home Screen.

1 Set up a phone

Once per phone. Takes a minute.

  1. Open skymath.org/c and tap Set up this device.
  2. Leave the backend on Private GitHub repo and fill in:
Ownerbrianpm
Repocellar-data
Branchmain
Pathdata
  1. Paste the Access token from the password manager.
  2. Set This device is called to something you’ll recognise — it shows up in the history of who changed what.
  3. Tap Save, then Verify. You want: “Connected to brianpm/cellar-data (private)”.
Never write the token on paper or send it in a message. It lives in the password manager, and it is pasted into each phone once.

2 Drink a bottle

  1. Point the camera at the sticker. Tap the link that pops up.
  2. Check it’s the right wine — the page shows the producer, the vintage, and which fridge and shelf it came from.
  3. Tap Drink this. Done; the bottle leaves the cellar list.
  4. Grabbed the wrong bottle? Undo appears at the bottom for ten seconds.
  5. You land on its archive entry. Add a note, tap a rating from 1 to 5, then Save note.

The note can wait. Open the bottle from Archive any time later — tomorrow, next year — and add one. Notes stack up.

Not drinking it? Other outcome… covers gifted, broken, corked, sold.
Changed your mind? Open it in Archive → Put back in the cellar.

3 Add new wine

  1. Open the app and tap Add wine.
  2. Fill in what you know — only the producer or the wine name is required. Add where you bought it and the price if you want the value and spending stats to mean anything.
  3. Set How many bottles, the fridge and the shelf. For a case, put 12 — you type the wine once. Tap Add.

Then label them. The app starts a labelling session and counts you through it:

  1. Stick a sticker on a bottle and scan it. The page says “Labelling session in progress — Bottle 3 of 12”.
  2. Tap Assign this label — one tap per bottle. Wrong wine on screen? Not this wine.

Stickers are blank until scanned, so any sticker fits any bottle. Keep a sheet near the fridges — an unlabelled bottle still counts, it just can’t be scanned yet.

? If something looks wrong

“Private cellar catalog” and nothing else — this phone hasn’t been set up. Go back to step 1.

“Label not recognized” — the sticker is genuine but this phone hasn’t caught up yet. Pull the page down to reload and try again; if it persists, that sticker was never registered.

“Token rejected” on the badge — the access token was replaced or expired. Paste the current one from the password manager into Settings again. Nothing is lost while this is broken; changes queue on the phone until it’s fixed.

Anything stranger than this: nothing you can do from the phone makes it worse. Every change is versioned and reversible.

Worth knowing

The badge at the top right is the save indicator:

Synced Syncing… 3 pending Offline — 3 pending Sync failed — tap

“Pending” is normal and clears itself in seconds. Only Sync failed wants attention — tap it to retry.

It works with no signal. Every tap is saved on the phone immediately and uploads when you’re back in range, so a basement with no bars changes nothing. Two people logging at once is fine; both are kept.

Sticker scratched or won’t scan? Type the six characters printed under the QR into the Look up box. The letters I L O U are never used — read them as 1, 1, 0.

Scanning a bottle you already drank shows its archive entry, tasting note and all — expected, not an error.

Guests see nothing. Someone who scans a bottle at dinner gets a blank page with the sticker’s code and nothing else — no wines, no prices.

Finding a bottle: the home screen lists every fridge shelf by shelf. Tonight suggests what’s ready; search takes a producer, a region or a sticker code.

Nothing is ever really deleted — every change is saved with a full history and can be undone. skymath.org/c
