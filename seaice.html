Arctic Sea Ice Volume

Monthly modeled Arctic sea ice volume from PIOMAS (Pan-Arctic Ice-Ocean Modeling and Assimilation System, UW Polar Science Center). Unlike the extent series above, this is a model reanalysis rather than a direct satellite measurement — see the note below on its current update status. There is no Southern Hemisphere counterpart on this page: Antarctic sea ice thickness is much harder to estimate from satellites because of deep, flooding snow cover, so no agency publishes a routine Antarctic volume time series. The same modeling group's GIOMAS project does cover the Antarctic, but only as raw gridded model output — see that page directly if you want to work with it.
